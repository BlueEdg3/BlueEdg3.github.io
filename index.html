Robotics

Micromouse PCB Design

3.3V LDOH-bridge driverIR arrayKiCad

Maze-solving Micromouse PCB with a four-pair IR emitter/receiver wall sensor array, a dual H-bridge motor driver with encoder inputs, and a switched 3.3V LDO. Sensor traces avoid the motor lines, and a separate MGND plane keeps motor return currents out of the logic.

PCB Design

UAV IMU Board

ATmega328P-ALSM303DIAM-70392FTDI + ICSP

Two-layer flight sensor board: ATmega328P-A at 3.3V with an LSM303D accelerometer/magnetometer (I2C) and an IAM-70392 gyroscope (SPI). FTDI auto-reset and ICSP headers give two programming paths, and bypass caps and a dedicated 3.3V rail keep the sensor bus clean.
